## Why run a diagnostic on a closed candidate

`within-session-expansion-v1.2` failed Stage 1 on its absolute coverage bar and is closed. That
does not change, whatever this run shows. It cannot be promoted, shipped, or reopened on this
evidence.

What the project has never measured is the premise under every expansion candidate since August:
that admitting more of an already-relevant session improves **answers**. v1.1 and v1.2 both stopped
at the free mechanism stage. v1.2's context passed every mechanism gate except the absolute bar,
which `2026-09-30-within-session-expansion-v1.2-stage1.md` records as calibrated against an obsolete
baseline. It is the best available instrument for the premise, and ~$6.50 buys the answer.
