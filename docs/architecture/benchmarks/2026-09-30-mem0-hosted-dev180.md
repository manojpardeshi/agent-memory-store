## Reading

Under an identical answer and judge protocol, raw retention outperforms Mem0 Hobby-tier extraction by
about 23 points, far outside the measured churn of about one answer per sixty. The largest gap is in
what the assistant said, 22/22 against 3/22: Mem0's extraction records facts about the user and drops
assistant-authored detail. Mem0 also trails on user facts, 25/26 against 17/26, which extraction
discards or compresses.

This is a measurement of Mem0's hosted Hobby tier under this protocol with three disclosed harness
patches. It supports no statement about Mem0's published 94.4%, other tiers, or other evaluators.
